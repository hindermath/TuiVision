## 011-port-wave2-examples

Datum: 2026-05-08.

OWASP SAMM bleibt als leichter Governance-Leitfaden anwendbar, aber es wird
kein vollstaendiges Feature-lokales SAMM-Reifegradassessment benoetigt. Die
konkreten Aktivitaeten sind: test-first Smoke-/Controls-Nachweise,
Dependency-Audit, Threat-Model-Notizen, Architektur-Risiken und
release-gebundene SBOM/VEX/SLSA-Entscheidung.

OWASP SAMM remains applicable as a lightweight governance guide, but this
feature does not need a full local SAMM maturity assessment. The concrete
activities are test-first smoke/controls evidence, dependency audit,
threat-model notes, architecture risks, and release-bound SBOM/VEX/SLSA
decision.
