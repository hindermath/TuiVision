## Implementation Strategy

### Reference slice first

1. Create evidence and provenance boundaries.
2. Make the absent canonical dataset fail explicitly.
3. Complete D02 end to end and prove that only incomplete domains remain red.
4. Expand inventory, decisions, secondary comparisons, and findings in that order.

### Incremental acceptance

1. US1 proves exact inventory ownership independently.
2. US2 proves historical intent and modern decisions independently.
3. US3 proves immutable secondary-source provenance independently.
4. US4 proves one-to-one findings and downstream suppression independently.
5. Final gates prove the evidence-only scope and remote delivery contract.

### Stop boundaries

- Stop on an unreachable pinned Free Vision commit, material governance conflict, public breaking-change decision, non-repairable required check, or a change that requires runtime implementation inside 024.
- Do not create Feature 025 or 026 unless the final accepted owner set is non-empty.
- Feature 027 remains mandatory after Feature 024 and any findings-driven remediation.
